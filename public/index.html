The Rules

        
Built like a case file,
run like a court.

      

      

        

          
RULE 01

          
The past is sealed

          
Yesterday's entries are read-only. Mark a task late and the record shows exactly what happened. No
            rewrites, no mercy.

        

        

          
RULE 02

          
Daily verdicts

          
Every day gets a completion percentage. Cross 80 percent and your streak lives. Fall short and the ledger
            turns red.

        

        

          
RULE 03

          
Emails that follow up

          
Your tasks arrive in your inbox every morning. Every evening, the verdict follows: completed, pending, and
            nowhere to hide.

        

        

          
RULE 04

          
One score above all

          
Completion, consistency and streaks roll into a single accountability score out of 100. It only respects
            sustained effort.

        

      

    

  


  

    

      

        
Procedure

        
Three steps. Zero loopholes.

      

      

        

          
01

          
Assignment

          
An admin assigns each task with an expected time and importance level. It applies from today onward,
            history stays untouched.

        

        

          
02

          
Execution

          
You get the list by email at dawn. Through the day you mark what is done. Only today's checkboxes can be
            touched.

        

        

          
03

          
Verdict

          
At night the ledger closes: completed, pending, percentage, and a message you may not enjoy. Then it is
            permanent.
